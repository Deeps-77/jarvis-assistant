# 🤖 Jarvis

Your private, **local-first** AI assistant — running entirely on this machine.

## What I can do

- 💬 **Answer anything** — searching the web when needed, with cited sources
- 🌤️ **Live facts, exactly** — weather anywhere, world clock & calendar math, exchange rates, crypto prices
- 📄 **Read your documents** — attach a PDF / DOCX / TXT / MD file and ask questions about it (private to you)
- 🎤 **Hear you speak** — use the mic button or attach audio; everything is transcribed locally

## Ground rules

- Answers are grounded in tool results and cited — if I can't verify something, I say so instead of guessing.
- Conversations and documents never leave this machine. No cloud AI APIs.

*Built with Ollama + LangGraph. Say hello!*
